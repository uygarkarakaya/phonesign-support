# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 26, 2026_

Phone Sign does not collect, store or share any personal data.

**On your device only.** The signs you type or draw, your saved signs and your settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

**No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.** Phone Sign has no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

**Purchases.** Phone Sign Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; the app only checks with Apple whether Pro has been purchased. We never receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information.

**Photos.** If you export a sign as a video and choose to save it, the app asks for permission to add that video to your Photos library. It cannot read or view your photos.

**Network.** The app works offline. It connects to Apple only for purchases, restoring purchases and App Store ratings.

**Children.** Phone Sign does not collect data from anyone, including children.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
